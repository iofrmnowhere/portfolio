Style Finder recommends stylistically similar outfits using a Gaussian Mixture
Model trained on the Polyvore Outfits dataset from HuggingFace. Its Streamlit
interface analyzes color and texture features from uploaded clothing images to
generate similar outfits. It was built by a team of four.
