Pop Hop is a full-stack flea market management web app for organizers and
vendors. It ships booth allocation, a management dashboard, and integrated
chat, secured with JWT-based role-based access control and backed by a
PostgreSQL database through Prisma ORM. It was built by a team of four using
Git workflows and deployed on Vercel.
